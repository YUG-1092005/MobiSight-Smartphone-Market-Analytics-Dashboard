# MobiSight - Smartphone Market Analytics Dashboard

## Project Overview
MobiSight is an interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze smartphone market trends, pricing strategy, brand positioning, feature adoption, and device specifications across different smartphone segments.

The project combines Python-based exploratory data analysis (EDA) with Power BI visual analytics to generate business-focused insights from smartphone market data.

---

# Objectives
- Analyze smartphone market distribution across brands and price segments
- Identify premium vs budget brand positioning
- Understand feature adoption trends such as 5G, fast charging, fingerprint sensor, and face unlock
- Compare smartphone specifications across different market segments
- Generate interactive business insights using Power BI

# ashboard Pages & Analysis

## 🔹 Summary Dashboard
Includes:
- Total Smartphones
- Average Price
- Average Battery
- Average RAM
- 5G Adoption %
- Brand Market Share
- Smartphone Launch Trend
- Price Segment Distribution

### Key Insights
- Mid-range smartphones dominate market volume
- Average smartphone price is around ₹38K
- 5G adoption is significantly high across the dataset

---

## 🔹 Brand Analysis
Includes:
- Brand Value vs Performance Analysis
- Brand Launch Velocity
- Brand Comparison Matrix

### Key Insights
- Apple leads premium pricing
- Xiaomi and Realme offer strong value-for-money positioning
- Samsung and Nothing maintains balanced pricing and performance positioning

---

## 🔹 Pricing & Segment Analysis
Includes:
- Market Segment Volume
- Specification Benchmark by Price Tier
- 5G Adoption by Price Segment
- 5G & Charging Technology Flow
- 5G Adoption with OS

### Key Insights
- Mid-range devices contribute the highest market share
- Mid-Range smartphones show higher 5G adoption
- Fast charging is strongly associated with 5G-enabled devices

---

## 🔹 Feature & Camera Analysis
Includes:
- Display Technology Distribution
- Refresh Rate vs Price
- Top Camera Setups
- Battery vs Fast Charging
- Camera Comparison by Brand

### Key Insights
- AMOLED displays dominate premium smartphones
- Higher refresh rates are associated with premium pricing
- Camera setups mostly have same distribution

---

## 🔹 Feature Adoption Analysis
Includes:
- Face Unlock Distribution
- Fingerprint Sensor Adoption
- Feature Adoption by Price Segment & OS

### Features Analyzed
- Dual SIM
- 5G Support
- Expandable Storage
- Water Resistance
- Wireless Charging
- Fingerprint Sensor
- Face Unlock

### Key Insights
- Fingerprint sensors are widely adopted across all segments
- Wireless charging and water resistance are concentrated in premium and ultra premium devices
- Android dominates overall smartphone market volume
- No expandable storage for IOS devices
